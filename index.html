Oxygen content vs. particle diameter

Model

A spherical particle of diameter D carries a uniform oxide shell of thickness t. With r = D/2, Vp = 4πr³/3, Vcore = 4π(r−t)³/3 and Vshell = Vp − Vcore:

O (wt.%) = 100 · ρoxVshell · fO / (ρMVcore + ρoxVshell), where fO = 2MO/(MM + 2MO) for MO₂.   SSA = 6/(ρM·D).

The original notebook (particle_ssa_oxygen.ipynb) uses ρMVp instead of ρMVcore for the metal mass, which counts the shell volume twice and gives slightly lower oxygen for fine powders.
